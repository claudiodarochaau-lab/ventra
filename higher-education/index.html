Why it matters

Amenity is part of the offer now.

Students compare campuses the way they compare everything else: by how it feels to be there every day. Staff notice what their employer puts in the common room. Coffee is small, visible, and judged constantly. Few investments this small do this much for how a campus feels every day, and few do this much damage when they fail.

Quality coffee in student spaces is one of the few amenity investments every student encounters every day they're on campus.

Campus amenity

Presence without a café build

A managed machine delivers café-quality coffee in libraries, kitchens and common rooms - without fit-out capital or staffing.

Procurement

One agreement to assess

Equipment, beans, service and reporting under a single agreement: one vendor to diligence, one invoice to process, one accountable partner.

Calendar-aware

Serviced to the academic year, not the calendar year.

A campus doesn't consume coffee like an office. Demand surges through teaching weeks, drops through breaks, and spikes at intake and exams. Ventra models volumes against your actual teaching calendar, so replenishment, maintenance windows and the commercial structure all follow the rhythm of your year.

  • Volume forecasts built on teaching weeks, not flat monthly averages
  • Preventative maintenance scheduled into breaks, not intake weeks
  • Monthly volume-versus-forecast reporting, so budgets hold no surprises

Bespoke per campus

There is no standard configuration.

Machine count, placement, capacity and the commercial structure are modelled for each campus individually - a single machine in a staff room and a multi-site, multi-campus deployment are both familiar structures for a Ventra agreement.

Your population

Staff and student numbers, teaching patterns and site traffic determine machine capacity - measured, not guessed.

Your spaces

Staff rooms, student commons, libraries, reception - placement follows how your campus actually moves.

Your structure

A per-site base fee plus a consumption component, shaped to your funding model and approval thresholds.
