Pricing

How pricing works.

Two components, no surprises: a per-site base fee and a consumption component, tailored to your campus and presented in full before anything is signed.

How is a managed coffee service priced?

Ventra pricing has two parts. A fixed per-site base fee covers the equipment, installation, filtration, telemetry, maintenance, reporting and management of the service. A consumption component scales with the cups actually poured, covering beans, milk and consumables. Together they appear as one monthly invoice.

The split matters. The base fee makes your cost predictable and keeps the service accountable to Ventra, not to your usage. The consumption component means a quiet campus in a teaching break isn't paying for coffee nobody drank.

How much does a managed coffee service cost for a campus?

It depends on four things: how many sites you're equipping, the machine capacity each site needs, your population, and your teaching calendar. We model all four for your campus and present the complete commercial picture, so you know your annual cost before you sign, not after.

Because volumes are forecast against your actual teaching weeks rather than flat monthly averages, the model holds through the year. Monthly volume-versus-forecast reporting keeps it honest in both directions.

Why is there no rate card?

Because no two campuses share a population, a calendar or a site count, a headline price built on averages would mislead more than it informs. A model built on your real numbers is more useful, and it's how we'd rather earn the engagement: by showing you the whole picture first.

How do multi-campus agreements work?

Each site carries its own base fee and consumption component, gathered under one agreement with one invoice, structured to suit your approval thresholds and funding model. Adding a site later extends the agreement; it doesn't restart it.
