The managed model · One page

Coffee, fully managed.

Equipment. Beans. Service. One partner, no downtime. The whole service, in order.

01Scope and design
Site survey covering power, water, placement and traffic. Volumes modelled against your population and operating calendar. Machine capacity matched to measured demand, with the full commercial model presented before anything is signed.
02Install and commission
Delivery and placement coordinated with your facilities team. Plumbing and commercial water filtration, commissioning and safety checks, and grind and recipe dial-in with our roasting partner. You sign off on a working service, not a delivered box.
03Beans and consumables
Specialty beans from Australian roaster Pablo & Rusty's, a certified B Corp with responsible sourcing through its supply chain. Milk, cups and cleaning consumables replenished on schedule, driven by actual consumption, before anything runs out.
04Servicing and Maintenance
Preventative servicing scheduled by Ventra, not requested by you. Day to day, a nominated coffee champion on your campus runs the daily clean and tops up beans and water between deliveries. We train them, and it takes minutes.
05Telemetry and reporting
Every machine reports in. Volumes, faults and maintenance windows are monitored remotely, and your nominated contact receives monthly volume-versus-forecast reporting. No surprises at renewal: you see the same data we do.
06One agreement, one invoice
Clear, transparent commercial terms, tailored to your institution and calendar. One agreement covers equipment, beans, service and reporting. Standard 30-day invoice terms.
