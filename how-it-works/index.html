How it works

The managed model, end to end.

Service anatomy

Six parts, one agreement.

01 · Scope and design

Every engagement starts with your site, not our brochure. We survey the space, model daily cup volumes against your population and calendar, and size equipment to real demand.

  • Site survey: power, water, placement, traffic flow
  • Volume modelling against your population and operating calendar
  • Machine sizing matched to measured demand - no over-speccing
  • Full commercial model presented before anything is signed
02 · Install and commission

Installation is ours from delivery to first cup. You sign off on a working service.

  • Delivery and placement, coordinated with your facilities team
  • Plumbing and commercial water filtration
  • Commissioning and safety checks
  • Grind and recipe dial-in with our roasting partner
03 · Beans and consumables

Specialty beans from an award-winning Australian roaster, a certified B Corp with responsible sourcing through its supply chain. Milk, cups and consumables are managed on schedule, replenished before they run out, not after.

  • Specialty roast profile selected for your machine and volume
  • Responsibly sourced through a certified B Corp roasting partner
  • Scheduled replenishment driven by actual consumption data
  • Milk, cups, lids and cleaning consumables included in the service
04 · Cleaning and maintenance

Preventative maintenance is scheduled by Ventra, not requested by you. Day to day, a nominated coffee champion on your campus runs the machine's one-button daily clean and keeps beans and water topped up between deliveries. We train them, and it takes minutes. We're open about this: those small daily tasks are part of how the service stays downtime-free and the coffee stays at its best.

  • One-button automated daily cleaning cycle, run by your campus coffee champion
  • Bean and water top-ups between scheduled deliveries, with our support
  • Scheduled preventative servicing
  • Fault response coordinated by Ventra with the equipment supplier's technician network
05 · Telemetry and reporting

Every machine reports in. We watch volumes, faults and maintenance windows remotely, which is how problems get fixed before they become downtime.

  • Remote monitoring of every installed machine
  • Monthly volume-versus-forecast reporting to your nominated contact
  • No surprises at renewal: you see the same data we do
06 · One agreement, one invoice

The commercial structure is a per-site base fee plus a consumption component, tailored to your institution. One agreement covers equipment, beans, service and reporting; one monthly invoice covers everything.

  • Per-site base fee plus consumption-based component
  • Term and structure negotiated per institution - not a fixed template
  • Standard 30-day invoice terms

The standard

Consistent, whoever pours it.

Dialled-in beans and a machine that doesn't drift over a service cycle mean the tenth cup of the day tastes like the first. That consistency is the actual product; everything in the anatomy above exists to protect it.

None of the six steps above came from a generic managed-services template. They were worked out backwards from the specific ways an unmanaged campus coffee supply breaks: beans running short during intake week, maintenance arriving mid-semester, fault reports sitting in a ticket queue while a machine sits idle. Calendar-aware scheduling, telemetry-triggered service response and volume forecasts built on teaching weeks - not flat monthly averages - are each a direct response to a pattern that repeats, reliably, when they're absent. Fifteen years inside Australian higher education is where that list was made.

What this replaces

We close the loop. Your team presses one button.

In an unmanaged setup, every step of the loop (noticing, reporting, ordering, chasing) runs through your staff. Under Ventra, the loop runs through telemetry and scheduled service. What stays on campus is deliberately light: a one-button daily clean and occasional top-ups by your coffee champion, keeping quality at its best between our visits.
