Frequently asked questions

What does a fully managed coffee service include?

A fully managed coffee service includes the equipment, specialty beans, milk and consumables, installation, cleaning, preventative maintenance, fault response and usage reporting — supplied by one partner under one agreement, with one monthly invoice.

With Ventra, the machine is monitored remotely by telemetry, replenishment is scheduled from actual consumption, and maintenance happens on our initiative. Your team's involvement ends at drinking the coffee. The full service anatomy sets out all six parts.

How does pricing work for a managed coffee service?

Ventra pricing has two parts: a fixed per-site base fee covering equipment, service and management, plus a consumption component that scales with the cups actually poured. The structure is tailored to each institution and presented in full before anything is signed.

We don't publish rate cards because no two campuses have the same population, calendar or site count — a model built on your real numbers is more useful than a headline price built on averages. Request a tailored proposal and we'll build one for your sites.

What equipment does Ventra use?

Ventra installs commercial Swiss-engineered superautomatic machines, sized to each site's measured daily volume, with commercial water filtration and telemetry fitted to every unit.

Superautomatic means barista-quality espresso and milk drinks at the touch of a button — no training, no staffing, consistent output all day. Machine capacity is matched to your population so you're never paying for headroom you don't use.

Where does the coffee come from?

Beans come from an award-winning Australian specialty roaster, with the roast profile selected for your machine and volume, and the grind and recipe dialled in on site, on your water.

Milk and alternative milks, cups, lids and cleaning consumables are all part of the managed supply — replenished on schedule, before anything runs out.

What happens when a machine needs service?

Usually, we know before you do. Every Ventra machine reports faults and usage remotely, so service is triggered by telemetry rather than by a complaint — and preventative maintenance is scheduled into your calendar's quiet weeks.

When a fault does occur, the response is ours to coordinate end to end. Nobody on your staff logs tickets, chases technicians or manages a warranty claim.

Does the service account for the academic calendar?

Yes — it's designed around it. Volume forecasts are built on teaching weeks rather than flat monthly averages, maintenance is scheduled into breaks, and reporting shows consumption against the forecast so budgets hold no surprises.

This is a direct product of the founder's fifteen years inside Australian higher education. Ventra for higher education covers the campus model in detail.

Where does Ventra operate?

Sydney and Melbourne, with multi-campus agreements available nationally for institutions operating across cities.

How does an engagement start?

With a thirty-minute discovery meeting. We scope your sites and population, model volumes against your calendar, and present the full commercial model — machine placement, capacity and pricing — before you're asked to commit to anything.
